Response surface

Cooling time as a function of ρ·Cp and k

The central panel reuses the second interface layout while introducing a 3D response surface for the cooling time model.

Model assumptions

ρ·Cp range: 800 – 2200 kJ/m³·K
k range: 0.2 – 1.6 W/m·K
Cooling time units: minutes
Model: conduction dominated
Adjust the ranges in the script if you want to test other materials or boundary conditions.

Interpretation guide

Higher ρ·Cp values typically slow cooling because more energy is stored per degree of temperature change.

A higher conductivity k accelerates cooling by improving heat flux through the material.

The surface combines these effects to highlight the minimum cooling band (darker region).

Key observations

Ridge at low k: Poor conductivity dominates and cooling time spikes.

Valley at moderate k: Optimal balance between capacity and conduction.

Plateau at high ρ·Cp: Material mass overwhelms conductive gains.
